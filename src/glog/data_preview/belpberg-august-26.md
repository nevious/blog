[Belpberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belpberg_(ridge)) (Belp Mountain), an isolated ridge in our local valley offers a very accessible hiking and mountain biking spot. We started our stroll in **Belp Mühlematt** and made our way up to the ridge, passing a Ruin from the 13th century. It's not at all well preserved and requires some light investigating to find its remnants.

The hill offers low difficulty [hiking trails](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAC-Wanderskala) (Yellow, T1), but not all paths are official trails and some may require a _slippery-when-wet_ sign. The use of a map or strictly sticking to the waymarks is recommended. Not all paths and forest roads are maintained. The end of the road near **Charholzbode** leads into a beaten path, full of thorns and dense bushes. Entirely manageable but not at all recommended. The path via **Elsisried** towards **Schönebode** should be more enjoyable.

Once you are on top of the ridge and keep heading south-east via the paved road, you'll be rewarded with a view of the [Bernese Alps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernese_Alps). Most prominently Eiger, Mönch & Jungfrau but also others like Niesen, Schreckhorn and Finsteraarhorn.
