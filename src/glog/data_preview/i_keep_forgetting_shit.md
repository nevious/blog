To avoid having browser tabs open idling away my limited resources and mental space, an improvement on tracking ideas is in order. Key aspects are decoupling from digital devices and locality. In short I want to be able to ensure remembering anything anywhere with little to no effort.

Assuming I suffer from the common issue of creativity striking me at the most inconvenient times it is reasonable to assume a common fix would help this. The inverse of creativity **not** striking when convenient is somewhat inferred.

Pretty much guaranteed, that as soon as there is some time to be wasted, nothing comes to mind. At this point it would be handy to be able to go back to things that once went through my head.

## What I hate

Easy - I hate my phone. Any phone really. Phones are distracting and attention demanding without even considering what runs **on** them. Yes, it can be configured to be silent and complacent but just as much as I hate the idea of educating my toaster I hate educating my phone about what it may and may not annoy me with.

The next idea would be Note apps like [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/), which I've tried resulting in an impressively fancy graveyard of half-assed notes, trying to make the most of the features it provides.

Digital tools do not seem to cut it for a couple of reasons:

- They are annoying (Phone)
- They are centralized in the most unhelpful way possible (Single File Notes)
- Any decentralization comes with unreasonable overhead (Git, syncing, more tooling)

Granted there are sub-use cases where these tools work perfectly fine. For my day to day work I do use [echaya/neowiki.nvim](https://github.com/echaya/neowiki.nvim) and it works a treat! However it is not applicable on the go.

## What might work

Short term memory is a preciously limited resource. I guess that's also the reason for this particular issue having been solved many-a-times before. But it has been made more complicated by all these fancy tools we have at our disposal. It's incredibly easy to take notes, however - to me, that is - it's incredibly hard to maintain them.

Obviously this is about taking physical notes in a small notebook - call it journaling for all I care - that can be carried easily along with other things I already carry. The context switch for taking a quick note by hand for easy retrieval later introduces only the limitation of being able to read my own writing. Few would consider this an obsolete skill but perhaps it is not.

I do not think that journaling alone is going to let me remember anything. After all the risk of having an analog rather than a digital graveyard seems indistinguishable. Since the goal of this is to let me prepare things to do when I have time to do them, some formal rules should help me. While limitation should be avoided, self guidance is perhaps a helpful tool.

- **Rule 1:** The journal is not to be abused as a todo list or personal organizer. There are plenty of tools to do this and nearly none of the things we tend to write there are of any consequence.
- **Rule 2:** The notebook should only contain things of personal value. Things that are inspiring, interesting or generally cause a positive mental state.
- **Rule 3:** Nothing in the notebook is compulsory. It is a place for things to be compiled and developed further. Things are not meant to be directly pulled from the notebook but are still allowed to.
- **Rule 4:** There is no form or structure to the notebook. Fighting chaos is a destructive limitation when it comes to creativity - embrace the chaos!

We'll see, what's at the end of this.
